      BDR playbook · built from the Incode BDR job description

      Open the call, earn the meeting, hand it to the AE

      Talk tracks, objection handling, a multichannel cadence, AI prompts and an event plan for an Incode BDR. Each part maps to a skill the role asks for: cold calling, account research, discovery, AI-enabled prospecting, events and AE partnership.

      30-second pitch. "Incode verifies that a person is real and is who they say they are, in seconds, for banks, telcos, marketplaces and governments. Most of our customers come to us for one of two reasons: AI fraud like deepfakes is getting past their current checks, or good customers are dropping out of sign-up. We've been named a Leader by Gartner three years running, and teams like yours use us to cut fraud without adding friction. Would it be crazy to compare notes for 15 minutes on how you handle that today?"

      Talk tracks by persona

      Lead with their problem, back it with one proof point, ask one good question, then ask for a short meeting.

      Bank or fintech

      Head of Fraud / Risk

      Opener
      "Hi Maria, it's Stefan from Incode. You weren't expecting my call, so I'll be quick: I'm calling fraud leaders because deepfakes and injected camera attacks are starting to pass selfie checks. Is that on your radar yet?"
      Proof
      Gartner Leader three years running; first to pass iBeta Level 3 liveness on iOS and Android; Deepsight runs under 100 ms per check.
      Discovery
      • How do you catch synthetic or injected identities today?
      • What share of fraud starts at onboarding versus account takeover?
      Ask
      "Worth 20 minutes with our team to see how your current checks score against a deepfake test?"
      Any enterprise

      CISO / Head of Security

      Opener
      "Hi James, Stefan from Incode. I'm calling security leaders about help-desk resets and fake job candidates, where someone with a deepfake gets an MFA reset or a job offer. Has that come up on your team?"
      Proof
      Incode Workforce plugs into Okta, Microsoft Entra, Ping and ServiceNow, with setup Incode says takes under an hour.
      Discovery
      • How does your help desk verify a caller before resetting MFA?
      • Do you check identity at interview or only at onboarding?
      Ask
      "Could I show you a 15-minute demo of a verified MFA reset in Okta?"
      Regulated business

      Compliance / KYC lead

      Opener
      "Hi Priya, Stefan from Incode. I work with KYC teams who need to meet KYC and AML rules without slowing sign-up. How are you handling PEP and sanctions screening in the same flow as ID checks?"
      Proof
      One flow for documents (4.9K+ types, 190+ countries), liveness, government records, and KYC/AML screening.
      Discovery
      • Which regulations or audits are driving priorities this year?
      • How many vendors are stitched together in your KYC flow?
      Ask
      "Open to a short call to map your flow against a single platform?"
      Marketplace, gaming, telco

      Head of Product / Growth

      Opener
      "Hi Alex, Stefan from Incode. I'm calling growth leaders because identity checks are often where good users drop off. Do you know your pass rate on the first try?"
      Proof
      Incode reports 98% of users verified on the first try and up to 20% conversion uplift when checking against government records.
      Discovery
      • Where in sign-up do you lose the most users?
      • How long does verification take today?
      Ask
      "Worth comparing your funnel numbers with ours in a 20-minute call?"

      Handling the usual objections

      Acknowledge, ask a question, then reframe. Log each objection in the desk so the Lessons tab shows which ones you hear most.

      "We already have a vendor."

      "Makes sense, most teams we talk to do. Out of curiosity, has your current setup been tested against deepfakes or injected cameras? Many teams run Incode side by side on a slice of traffic to compare. Would a benchmark be useful when your contract comes up?"

      "Just send me an email."

      "Happy to. So I send something useful and not a brochure: is your bigger worry fraud getting through, or real users dropping out? … Great, I'll send that, and can I put 15 minutes on Thursday to walk through it?"

      "No budget right now."

      "Understood. Budget usually shows up after a fraud spike or an audit finding. What would fraud losses or failed sign-ups need to look like for this to become a priority? I can share how others built the business case."

      "Not my area."

      "Thanks for telling me. Who owns identity verification there: fraud, compliance or product? Would you mind if I mention you pointed me their way?"

      "We built it in-house."

      "Impressive. How are you keeping up with new attack types like injected video? Many in-house teams use Incode as the liveness and deepfake layer under their own flow rather than replacing it."

      "Bad timing, call me next quarter."

      "Will do. What happens next quarter that makes it a better time? … I'll call on the 3rd. Mind if I send one short case study before then?" Log the date as the next step.

      AI prompt kit for prospecting

      The role asks for an AI-forward mindset. Paste these into Claude with a company or person filled in. Always check facts before using them on a call.

      Account research

      Research [Company] for an identity verification sales call. Give me: what they sell and to whom, recent news (funding, launches, fraud incidents, regulatory actions) from the last 12 months, which regulations likely apply (KYC, AML, age verification), who probably owns fraud, compliance and security, and 3 reasons they might need better identity verification now. Flag anything you are unsure of.

      Personalized opener

      Write a 2-sentence cold call opener for [Name], [Title] at [Company]. Reference [trigger, e.g. their new app launch]. Connect it to one problem Incode solves (deepfakes, sign-up drop-off, KYC compliance or help-desk fraud). Sound like a person, not a script. Then write one discovery question to ask next.

      Email that gets replies

      Write a cold email under 90 words to [Name], [Title] at [Company]. Subject line under 5 words. Open with their likely problem, add one proof point (Gartner Leader three years running, or 98% verified on the first try), and end with a soft question, not a meeting link. No buzzwords.

      Call recap for the AE

      Turn these call notes into a handoff for my Account Executive: [notes]. Format: company and contact, the pain in their words, BANT status (budget, authority, need, timing) with evidence, objections raised, agreed next step and date, and suggested angle for the first meeting.

      Account tiering

      Account research and prioritization: spend most calls on Tier A. Set the tier on each prospect in the desk.

      Tier A · call this week

      • Regulated industry with high sign-up volume (bank, fintech, telco, gaming, marketplace)
      • A trigger: fraud incident, new regulation, app launch, funding, vendor renewal
      • A named fraud, compliance or security leader you can reach

      Tier B · work in cadence

      • Right industry and size, but no trigger yet
      • Contact known but not the decision maker
      • Nurture with content: case studies, Deepfakes 101

      Tier C · park

      • Low volume or no regulatory pressure
      • Looking for a free or self-serve tool
      • Revisit each quarter or after a trigger

      Event and networking plan

      For Money20/20, Decoded and industry dinners.

      Before

      Book meetings early

      • Pull the attendee and speaker list; tier the accounts
      • Email Tier A two weeks out: invite them to the booth or the Deepfake Guesser
      • Agree with your AE who covers which accounts
      During

      Start conversations

      • Use the Deepfake Guesser as the opener: "Can you spot the fake?"
      • Ask one discovery question, take a photo of the badge
      • Log each conversation in the desk the same day, type "Event"
      After

      Follow up in 48 hours

      • Personal email referencing what you talked about
      • Connect on LinkedIn with a note
      • Book the meeting or set a dated next step
